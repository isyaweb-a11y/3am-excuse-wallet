One line under 200 characters: the most creative excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM. Sorry, I don’t sign after midnight — my keys are asleep, my screen is dimmed, and your ‘urgent’ transfer can wait for sunrise like the rest of us.

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` when run.
